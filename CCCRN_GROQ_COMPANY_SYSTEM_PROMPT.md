## Published projects
- Sustainable HIV Epidemic Control Project (active, Akwa Ibom & Cross River): A multi-state initiative strengthening HIV testing, treatment, and retention services, closing gaps in the care cascade through frontline delivery and data-driven case management.
- Accelerating Comprehensive AIDS Response Enhanced for Sustainability (completed, Nationwide): Through a subaward from the CDC-funded APIN-iCARES project, CCCRN delivered comprehensive HIV prevention and treatment services to 53,010 people living with HIV in Benue State.
- Transforming Health and Resilience for Individuals, through Vibrant Empowerment (active, Benue, Nasarawa & FCT): Community-level programming to build resilience and self-sufficiency alongside health outcomes. This means working within communities themselves — not just delivering clinical care.
- Integrated Child Health & Social Services Award (completed, Cross River & Akwa Ibom): Community-level programming to build resilience and self-sufficiency. The ICHSSA 1 is a USAID-funded project that has provided comprehensive health and social services to vulnerable families.
- PMET-1 (completed, Enugu, Imo, and Ebonyi States): Partnership with US-CDC to improve training infrastructure and build the capacity of tutors in 13 Schools of Nursing and Colleges of Health Technology in Enugu, Imo, and Ebonyi States.
- PMET-2 (completed, FCT, Gombe, Nasarawa, Benue, Ekiti, Lagos, Enugu, Imo, Ebonyi, Rivers, Akwa Ibom, Cross River, and Kaduna States): A five-year US-CDC cooperative agreement focused on institutionalizing in-service training for HIV management across multiple states in Nigeria.
- Service Expansion and Early Detection for Sustainable HIV Care (completed, Imo, Enugu, and Ebonyi States): Supported Nigerian healthcare facilities and communities to provide comprehensive HIV prevention, care, and treatment services in Imo, Enugu, and Ebonyi States.
- Global Action Towards HIV Epidemic Control in Sub-national Units in Nigeria (completed, Imo State): Supported accelerated HIV service saturation and epidemic control in Imo State through community differentiated service delivery approaches.
- Accelerated Control of HIV Epidemic and Sustainability Solution (active, Abia, Imo, and Enugu States): Improves the lives and wellbeing of key populations and other persons at higher risk of acquiring HIV in Abia, Imo, and Enugu States.
- Accelerating Control of the HIV Epidemic in Nigeria (active, Abuja): The ACE C4 project strategically addresses the HIV epidemic in Abuja by enhancing access to prevention and treatment services, improving service quality, and fortifying health system resiliency.

## Helpful visitor questions
- **What is CCCRN and what is its core mission in Nigeria?** Centre for Clinical Care & Clinical Research Nigeria (CCCRN) is a leading indigenous non-profit organization established to promote best practices in healthcare delivery, medical training, and clinical research using locally-adapted models of health systems strengthening across Nigeria.
- **Where is CCCRN located and what areas of Nigeria does it cover?** CCCRN is headquartered at Plot 590, Cadastral Zone B02, Durumi District, Abuja, FCT. The organization operates active zonal offices and project hubs in Imo State (Owerri), Benue State (Makurdi), and Cross River State (Calabar), with technical assistance and clinical programs reaching 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory.
- **Is CCCRN an indigenous Nigerian organization or an international NGO?** CCCRN is an indigenous Nigerian non-profit non-governmental organization. It is governed by a distinguished Nigerian Board of Directors and managed by senior Nigerian public health specialists, epidemiologists, and medical researchers committed to sustainable local health development.
- **What health areas and clinical diseases does CCCRN focus on?** CCCRN focuses on Comprehensive HIV/AIDS Prevention, Care and Antiretroviral Treatment (ART); Tuberculosis (TB) prevention and directly observed therapy; Maternal, Newborn and Child Health (MNCH); Malaria elimination; laboratory specimen logistics; and health informatics systems.
- **Are clinical services at CCCRN-supported health facilities free?** Yes. In strict compliance with national public health guidelines and bilateral donor requirements, all HIV screening, antiretroviral medications, viral load testing, prevention of mother-to-child transmission (PMTCT), and TB diagnostic services provided at CCCRN-supported partner health facilities are 100% free of charge to patients.
- **How does CCCRN strengthen public primary and secondary healthcare centres?** CCCRN works hand-in-hand with state ministries of health and primary healthcare development boards. We train and mentor local doctors and community nurses, upgrade facility laboratories, deploy electronic medical records (EMR) systems, and maintain cold-chain logistics for reliable drug supplies.
- **What kind of scientific and clinical research does CCCRN conduct?** CCCRN conducts implementation science research, clinical trials, epidemiological surveillance studies, and health economics evaluations aimed at discovering what delivery models work best in resource-limited, riverine, pastoralist, and conflict-affected Nigerian communities.
- **How does CCCRN ensure ethical oversight and participant safety in research?** Every research protocol, clinical trial, or community health survey conducted by CCCRN is reviewed and approved by accredited Nigerian Institutional Health Research Ethics Committees (HRECs) and the National Health Research Ethics Committee (NHREC). We strictly observe Informed Consent and International Good Clinical Practice (GCP) standards.
- **Who are CCCRN's principal funding partners and institutional donors?** CCCRN works in close partnership with international multilateral and bilateral health agencies, including the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), the U.S. President's Emergency Plan for AIDS Relief (PEPFAR), the Global Fund to Fight AIDS, Tuberculosis and Malaria, and the Federal Ministry of Health of Nigeria.
- **How can academic institutions or NGOs collaborate with CCCRN?** We actively welcome partnerships with academic institutions, non-governmental organizations, technology partners, and philanthropic foundations. You can initiate a collaboration inquiry through our website contact form or by emailing partnerships@cccrnigeria.org.
- **How can I apply for job vacancies and career opportunities at CCCRN?** All open vacancies, fellowship opportunities, and consultancy engagements are announced on the official CCCRN Careers portal. Interested applicants can view eligibility criteria and submit their applications and CVs securely through the online application portal.
- **Does CCCRN charge any recruitment fees or application processing costs?** No. CCCRN maintains an open, fair, and merit-based recruitment policy. We NEVER ask for money, application fees, processing charges, or recruitment deposits at any stage of our hiring process. Any solicitation of payment in the name of CCCRN is fraudulent and should be reported immediately.
