**Q1. What is CCCRN and what is its core mission in Nigeria?**

Centre for Clinical Care & Clinical Research Nigeria (CCCRN) is a leading indigenous non-profit organization established to promote best practices in healthcare delivery, medical training, and clinical research using locally-adapted models of health systems strengthening across Nigeria.

**Q2. Where is CCCRN located and what areas of Nigeria does it cover?**

CCCRN is headquartered at Plot 590, Cadastral Zone B02, Durumi District, Abuja, FCT. The organization operates active zonal offices and project hubs in Imo State (Owerri), Benue State (Makurdi), and Cross River State (Calabar), with technical assistance and clinical programs reaching 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory.

**Q3. Is CCCRN an indigenous Nigerian organization or an international NGO?**

CCCRN is an indigenous Nigerian non-profit non-governmental organization. It is governed by a distinguished Nigerian Board of Directors and managed by senior Nigerian public health specialists, epidemiologists, and medical researchers committed to sustainable local health development.

**Q4. What health areas and clinical diseases does CCCRN focus on?**

CCCRN focuses on Comprehensive HIV/AIDS Prevention, Care and Antiretroviral Treatment (ART); Tuberculosis (TB) prevention and directly observed therapy; Maternal, Newborn and Child Health (MNCH); Malaria elimination; laboratory specimen logistics; and health informatics systems.

**Q5. Are clinical services at CCCRN-supported health facilities free?**

Yes. In strict compliance with national public health guidelines and bilateral donor requirements, all HIV screening, antiretroviral medications, viral load testing, prevention of mother-to-child transmission (PMTCT), and TB diagnostic services provided at CCCRN-supported partner health facilities are 100% free of charge to patients.

**Q6. How does CCCRN strengthen public primary and secondary healthcare centres?**

CCCRN works hand-in-hand with state ministries of health and primary healthcare development boards. We train and mentor local doctors and community nurses, upgrade facility laboratories, deploy electronic medical records (EMR) systems, and maintain cold-chain logistics for reliable drug supplies.

**Q7. What kind of scientific and clinical research does CCCRN conduct?**

CCCRN conducts implementation science research, clinical trials, epidemiological surveillance studies, and health economics evaluations aimed at discovering what delivery models work best in resource-limited, riverine, pastoralist, and conflict-affected Nigerian communities.

**Q8. How does CCCRN ensure ethical oversight and participant safety in research?**

Every research protocol, clinical trial, or community health survey conducted by CCCRN is reviewed and approved by accredited Nigerian Institutional Health Research Ethics Committees (HRECs) and the National Health Research Ethics Committee (NHREC). We strictly observe Informed Consent and International Good Clinical Practice (GCP) standards.

**Q9. Who are CCCRN's principal funding partners and institutional donors?**

CCCRN works in close partnership with international multilateral and bilateral health agencies, including the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), the U.S. President's Emergency Plan for AIDS Relief (PEPFAR), the Global Fund to Fight AIDS, Tuberculosis and Malaria, and the Federal Ministry of Health of Nigeria.

**Q10. How can academic institutions or NGOs collaborate with CCCRN?**

We actively welcome partnerships with academic institutions, non-governmental organizations, technology partners, and philanthropic foundations. You can initiate a collaboration inquiry through our website contact form or by emailing partnerships@cccrnigeria.org.

**Q12. Does CCCRN charge any recruitment fees or application processing costs?**

No. CCCRN maintains an open, fair, and merit-based recruitment policy. We NEVER ask for money, application fees, processing charges, or recruitment deposits at any stage of our hiring process. Any solicitation of payment in the name of CCCRN is fraudulent and should be reported immediately.

The **Centre for Clinical Care & Clinical Research Nigeria (CCCRN)** is dedicated to safeguarding the personal privacy, fundamental rights, and data dignity of every individual who interacts with our organization. This Privacy Policy delineates how CCCRN collects, processes, protects, and discloses personal and health data in strict compliance with the **Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023)**, the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), the National Code for Health Research Ethics, and applicable international privacy standards.

This policy applies to all visitors to our website, program participants, job applicants, donors, partners, and digital portal users.

---

## 1. Principles of Data Protection

In handling personal data, CCCRN adheres rigorously to the foundational data protection principles codified in Section 24 of the NDPA 2023:

* **Lawfulness, Fairness & Transparency:** Personal data is collected and processed with clear legal justification, in good faith, and with open disclosure to data subjects.
* **Purpose Limitation:** Information is collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate public health and organizational purposes and not processed in ways incompatible with those purposes.
* **Data Minimization:** We only collect information that is adequate, relevant, and strictly necessary for stated activities.
* **Accuracy:** We implement reasonable controls to ensure personal data is accurate, complete, and kept up to date.
* **Storage Limitation:** Personal data is retained only for the duration necessary to accomplish the intended purpose or to satisfy statutory and ethical record-keeping mandates.
* **Integrity & Confidentiality:** Data is safeguarded against unauthorized or unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction, or compromise using state-of-the-art technological and organizational measures.

## 2. Categories of Information We Collect

### A. Information You Voluntarily Provide
* **General Inquiries & Feedback:** Full name, email address, telephone number, organization, and the content of your message when you complete our public contact forms.
* **Employment & Fellowship Applications:** Curriculum vitae (CV), academic transcripts, professional medical registrations, employment history, identity verification documents, referee contact details, and applicant demographic details submitted via our Careers portal.
* **Partnership & Grants Correspondence:** Institutional details, designated representative information, official proposals, and contractual contact information.
* **Charitable Donations:** Donor name, billing address, transaction receipts, and payment references (payment card details are processed directly through PCI-DSS compliant financial gateways and are never stored on CCCRN servers).

### B. Technical & Device Information Automatically Collected
* Internet Protocol (IP) address, approximate geolocation, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, pages viewed, session duration, and crash log telemetry collected to maintain server stability and site security.

### C. Clinical & Public Health Research Data
* Clinical health records, laboratory test metrics, and epidemiological observations collected in our supported facilities are processed under stringent biomedical confidentiality protocols. Public-facing platforms display **only aggregated, fully anonymized, or de-identified datasets** from which individual participant identities cannot be reverse-engineered.

## 3. Lawful Bases for Processing

In accordance with Section 25 of the NDPA 2023, CCCRN processes your personal data under one or more of the following legal grounds:

1. **Consent:** Where you have granted clear, explicit consent for a specific processing purpose (e.g., newsletter subscriptions, contact inquiries, application submissions).
2. **Performance of a Contract:** Where processing is required to execute an employment contract, grant agreement, procurement order, or institutional partnership.
3. **Compliance with Legal Obligations:** Where processing is mandated by Nigerian statutory laws, public health reporting frameworks, or financial auditing requirements.
4. **Public Interest in Health & Epidemiology:** For reasons of public interest in the area of public health, such as protecting against serious cross-border threats to health, monitoring epidemic diseases, or maintaining clinical standards of medicinal care.
5. **Legitimate Interests:** Where processing is necessary for the legitimate operational, administrative, or analytical needs of CCCRN, without overriding your fundamental privacy rights.

CCCRN employs defense-in-depth technical, physical, and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure:

## 9. Your Rights as a Data Subject (NDPA 2023)

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you are endowed with fundamental rights concerning your personal data, which you may exercise at any time without fee or prejudice:

1. **Right of Access:** You have the right to request confirmation of whether we process your personal data and to obtain a copy of such data.
2. **Right to Rectification:** You may request the correction of incomplete, out-of-date, or inaccurate personal information.
3. **Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten):** You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data where retention is no longer legally justified.
4. **Right to Restrict Processing:** You may request that we temporarily suspend processing of your data under specific statutory conditions.
5. **Right to Data Portability:** You may request a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable copy of your data for transfer to another controller.
6. **Right to Object:** You may object to data processing based on legitimate interests or direct communications.
7. **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Where processing is founded on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time without impacting prior lawful processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please submit a written request to our Data Protection Officer at `dpo@cccrnigeria.org`. We will respond within thirty (30) days as stipulated by the NDPA.
